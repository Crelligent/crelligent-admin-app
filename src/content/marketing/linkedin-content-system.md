# LinkedIn Content System
## 30-Day Content Templates & Frameworks

---

## Content Pillar Breakdown

| Pillar | Description | Post Types | Frequency |
|--------|-------------|------------|-----------|
| 🎯 **Stories** | Personal journey, lessons, behind-the-scenes | Origin, Failure, Win, Reflection | 2x/week |
| 📚 **Education** | Teach something valuable | Listicle, Framework, How-to, Myth-buster | 2x/week |
| 🏆 **Social Proof** | Results, testimonials, case studies | Case Study, Testimonial, Data | 1x/week |
| 🚀 **Promotion** | Drive signups, sales, engagement | Launch, Giveaway, CTA, Event | 1x/week |
| 💬 **Engagement** | Spark conversation, build community | Question, Poll, Hot Take | 1x/week |

---

## 30 Post Templates

### 🎯 STORY POSTS

---

#### Template 1: The Origin Story

```
I started [company/project] because of one moment.

[Describe the specific moment that triggered everything]

Before that, I was [previous situation].

But after seeing [the problem firsthand], I couldn't unsee it.

So I decided to [what you did].

[X months/years] later:
• [Result 1]
• [Result 2]
• [Result 3]

The lesson?

[One-line insight]

What moment changed your path? 👇
```

---

#### Template 2: The Failure Story

```
My biggest failure taught me everything.

In [year], I [what you tried to do].

I was confident. I had [resources/plan/team].

But then [what went wrong].

The result?
→ [Negative outcome 1]
→ [Negative outcome 2]
→ [Negative outcome 3]

It was painful. But here's what I learned:

1. [Lesson 1]
2. [Lesson 2]
3. [Lesson 3]

Today, I [how you apply those lessons].

Failure isn't the opposite of success—it's part of it.

What failure taught you the most?
```
