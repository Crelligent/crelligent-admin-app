## Day 5: The Hot Take (Controversial/Engaging)

```
Unpopular opinion:

Most startups don't need a mobile app.

There. I said it.

Before you spend 6 months and ₦10M building an app:

→ Can you validate with a simple website?
→ Do your users actually prefer mobile?
→ Is there enough repeat usage to justify it?

I've seen founders build apps that get 50 downloads.
And websites that get 10,000 monthly visitors.

Start where your users are.
Not where you think they should be.

Agree or disagree? Let me know below. 👇
```
