# Crelligent "Systems in Motion" Infographic Brief

## Purpose
LinkedIn intro post infographic explaining the Crelligent logo meaning and brand philosophy.

---

## Visual Concept

### Main Element: The Systems Sphere
A dynamic sphere made of **4 flowing, interconnected ribbons**, each representing a practice area:

| Ribbon | Color Gradient | Label | Icon |
|--------|----------------|-------|------|
| 1 | Red → Orange | **STRATEGY** | 💡 Lightbulb or ♟️ Chess piece |
| 2 | Yellow → Green | **TECHNOLOGY** | ⚙️ Gear or `</>` Code |
| 3 | Teal → Blue | **EXPERIENCE** | ❤️ Heart or 🎨 Palette |
| 4 | Blue → Purple | **OPERATIONS** | 🚀 Rocket or 📈 Chart |

### Flow Arrows
Subtle arrows showing the circular flow:
```
Strategy → Technology → Experience → Operations → (back to Strategy)
```

### Text Elements
- **Center/Top:** "Systems in Motion"
- **Bottom Tagline:** "Think in systems. Build with precision."
- **Optional:** Small "CRELLIGENT & CO." wordmark

---

## Style

- **Aesthetic:** Modern, friendly, professional cartoon illustration
- **Colors:** Vibrant gradients matching the logo (rainbow spectrum)
- **Background:** Clean white or very light gray
- **Lines:** Smooth, flowing curves (not rigid/geometric)
- **Icons:** Simple, flat, playful
