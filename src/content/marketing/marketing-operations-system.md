## 2. Content System

### Content Pillars

| Pillar | Purpose | % of Content |
|--------|---------|--------------|
| **Stories** | Build connection, show journey | 25% |
| **Education** | Provide value, establish expertise | 35% |
| **Social Proof** | Build trust, show results | 20% |
| **Promotion** | Drive conversions | 20% |

### Content Calendar Structure

| Day | Monday | Tuesday | Wednesday | Thursday | Friday |
|-----|--------|---------|-----------|----------|--------|
| Type | Education | Story | Social Proof | Education | Promotion |
| Format | Listicle | Personal | Case Study | Framework | CTA |

### Content Workflow

```
1. IDEATE (Sunday)
   → Review content pillars
   → Check trending topics
   → Draft 5 post ideas

2. CREATE (Monday-Tuesday)
   → Write posts using templates
   → Create graphics if needed
   → Schedule in LinkedIn

3. PUBLISH (Daily @ 8-9 AM WAT)
   → Post goes live
   → Set 30-min reminder to engage

4. ENGAGE (Within 2 hours)
   → Reply to all comments
   → Like and comment on others' posts
   → DM anyone who engages meaningfully

5. ANALYZE (Weekly - Friday)
   → Check impressions, engagement
   → Identify top performers
   → Adjust next week's strategy
```

## 7. Standard Operating Procedures

### SOP 1: New Lead Response

**Trigger:** New lead books strategy call  
**Timeline:** Within 2 hours

1. Send confirmation email (auto)
2. Add to CRM with source tag
3. Research their company (5 min)
4. Prepare 3 questions for the call
5. Send LinkedIn connection request

### SOP 2: Content Publishing

**Trigger:** Scheduled post day  
**Timeline:** 8-9 AM WAT

1. Open scheduled post
2. Final proofread
3. Add relevant hashtags (3-5 max)
4. Publish
5. Engage with 5 other posts immediately
6. Return in 30 min to respond to comments

### SOP 3: Weekly Content Planning

**Trigger:** Every Sunday  
**Timeline:** 60-90 minutes

1. Review last week's performance (15 min)
2. Identify top-performing content types
3. Draft 5 post outlines for the week
4. Write 2-3 full posts
5. Schedule in content calendar
6. Create any needed graphics

### SOP 4: Partnership Outreach

**Trigger:** Partnership target identified  
**Timeline:** Within 24 hours

1. Research the partner (company, person, audience)
2. Find mutual connection or shared interest
3. Draft personalized outreach message
4. Send via LinkedIn DM or email
5. Follow up after 5 days if no response
6. Log in CRM

## 8. Quality Standards

### Content Quality Checklist

- [ ] Hook in first line?
- [ ] Single clear message?
- [ ] Value for reader?
- [ ] Easy to scan (short paragraphs)?
- [ ] Clear CTA at the end?
- [ ] No typos or errors?
- [ ] Authentic voice?

### Email Quality Checklist

- [ ] Subject line < 50 characters?
- [ ] Personalized greeting?
- [ ] One clear call-to-action?
- [ ] Mobile-friendly format?
- [ ] Unsubscribe link visible?
- [ ] Sent at optimal time?

---

## 9. Emergency Playbook

### Scenario: Content not performing

1. Analyze: What's different from top performers?
2. Test: Try different hook or format
3. Engage: Spend more time commenting on others
4. Adjust: Shift content pillar mix

### Scenario: Low lead flow

1. Check website analytics for traffic drop
2. Review email open rates
3. Increase posting frequency
4. Run a giveaway or free resource
5. Activate partnership network

### Scenario: Negative feedback

1. Respond professionally within 1 hour
2. Take conversation private (DM)
3. Resolve the issue
4. Document learnings
5. Improve process
