# Crelligent — CMO Master Marketing & Go-To-Market Strategy

> **Prepared by:** Chief Marketing Officer, Crelligent
> **Date:** February 15, 2026
> **Mission:** Position Crelligent as Africa's premier Systems Design & Engineering firm, and drive revenue across three verticals — Foundry, Embedded Intelligence, and Enterprise — through disciplined, engineering-grade marketing.

---

## Executive Summary

Crelligent is not an agency, a dev shop, or a consultancy. We are a **Systems Design & Engineering firm** that operates across three interconnected system domains: Software Systems, IT/Enterprise Systems, and Business Systems. We serve three market verticals (Foundry, Embedded Intelligence, Enterprise), powered by three proprietary platforms (VeloDesk, MarketPulse, BI Suite), through nine integrated capabilities.

This GTM strategy treats marketing itself as an engineered system — with clear inputs, feedback loops, channels, and measurable outputs — aligned to how we build for our clients.

> *For organizations that have outgrown their systems — we redesign what's underneath.*

#### Value Proposition
Enterprise systems accumulate debt. We apply the ESRE (Enterprise System Redesign Engagement)™ to diagnose, architect, build, and evolve organizational systems.

#### The ESRE™ Framework (Flagship Engagement)

| Phase | Duration | What We Do | Deliverables |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Diagnostic** | 4–6 weeks | Executive interviews, system mapping, signal review | Enterprise System Map, Constraint Analysis, Risk Brief |
| **2. Architecture** | 6–10 weeks | Business system redesign, data/decision design | Target Architecture, Experience Blueprint, Platform Roadmap |
| **3. Build** | 12–24 weeks | Platform engineering, CX implementation, change enablement | Working systems, operational dashboards, trained teams |
| **4. Evolution** | Ongoing | Quarterly reviews, feedback loops, resilience assessments | Health Reports, Improvement Backlog, Foresight Briefings |

#### Revenue Model
- **Diagnostic Only:** $50K–$100K (standalone assessment)
- **Full ESRE™ Engagement:** $150K–$500K+ (phases 1–3)
- **Ongoing Evolution Retainer:** $10K–$25K/month
- **BI Suite Licensing:** Per-seat SaaS revenue

| **The Overwhelmed CTO** | Legacy systems, technical debt, tool sprawl | "Everything is duct-taped together" |

| **The PE Portfolio Manager** | Needs to rationalize operations post-acquisition | "We need to integrate 3 companies" |
