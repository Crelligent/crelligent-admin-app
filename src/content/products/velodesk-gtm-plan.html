01 Executive Summary

Velodesk is the first automated "Product-Market Fit Score" for startups. By standardizing PMF into a verifiable metric (0-100), we bridge the information gap between founders and capital. This document outlines the strategy to scale from $0 to $100M ARR by leveraging high-leverage distribution nodes: VCs and Accelerators.

02 Market Opportunity

  • The Pain Point: Founders lack objective proof of growth; VCs lack real-time visibility into portfolio health.
  • The Solution: A data-driven score powered by 40+ integrations (Stripe, Segment, HubSpot).
  • TAM: $50B+ global spend on startup analytical tools and fundraising software.

03 GTM Phases

Phase 1: Brand Standard (Seed)

Focus on getting the "Velodesk Badge" on 1,000+ top startup landing pages. This creates a viral "Lighthouse Effect" where investors see the badge and ask other founders for their score.

Phase 2: Distribution Nodes (Scale)

Direct partnerships with accelerators (YC, Techstars, Antler). We provide a unified dashboard for program managers in exchange for onboarding their entire batch onto our paid growth tier.
