# Velodesk: The Path to $100M ARR
## Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy & Execution Plan

---

## 1. Executive Summary
Velodesk is the first "Credit Score" for startups. By standardizing Product-Market Fit (PMF) into a single, verifiable score (0-100), we eliminate the ambiguity in early-stage fundraising. This plan outlines how we will scale from $0 to $100M ARR by leveraging VCs and accelerators as distribution engines.

---

## 2. Market Opportunity
*   **The Problem:** Founders don't know if they have PMF; Investors don't have an objective way to measure it until it's too late.
*   **The Solution:** An automated, multi-integration platform that calculates "The PMF Score" using real data (Stripe, Mixpanel, etc.).
*   **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** 100+ million new startups founded globally every year.
*   **Target:** $100M ARR within 5 years.

---

## 3. Go-To-Market Strategy

### Phase 1: The "Seed" Phase (0 - $1M ARR)
*   **Focus:** Founders & Personal Brand.
*   **Channel:** Product Hunt, LinkedIn, and X.
*   **Objective:** Get 1,000 "Verified" founders to display the Velodesk Badge on their landing pages.
*   **Viral Loop:** Every founder who shares a report or displays a badge drives more founders to the site.

### Phase 2: The "Scale" Phase ($1M - $10M ARR)
*   **Focus:** VCs & Accelerators (Distribution Partners).
*   **Mechanism:** Partner with tier-1 accelerators to make Velodesk the "Mandatory Dashboard" for their portfolio.
*   **Revenue:** Transition from Founder subscriptions to "Investor Portfolio Licenses."

### Phase 3: The "Standard" Phase ($10M - $100M ARR)
*   **Focus:** Global Standard.
*   **Objective:** Become the "FICO Score" of the startup world.
*   **Enterprise:** White-label solutions for M&A firms and large corp-dev teams.

---

## 4. Accelerator Partnership Proposal

### Value Proposition for Accelerators (YC, Techstars, etc.)
1.  **Objective Portfolio Health:** Track all portfolio companies in one real-time dashboard.
2.  **Early Warning System:** Identify "Churn Risk" or "Revenue Stall" before month-end reports.
3.  **Demo Day Prep:** Give founders a "Velodesk Verified" stamp for their pitch decks to increase valuation.

### The Offer
*   **Free for Batch Companies:** All startups in the current batch get 6 months of Velodesk Growth for free.
*   **Accelerator Admin Dashboard:** Full visibility for program managers.
