## Part 2: Contract Templates for Your Lawyer

### Client-Facing Contracts

| # | Template | Purpose | When Used |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Master Service Agreement (MSA)** | Governs the overall relationship with a client | Signed once per client, before any work begins |
| 2 | **Statement of Work (SOW)** | Defines scope, deliverables, timeline, price for a specific engagement | Signed per engagement (attached to the MSA) |
| 3 | **Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)** | Protects confidential information shared during discovery/engagement | Signed before sharing sensitive business information |
| 4 | **Change Order Form** | Formalizes scope changes and additional costs mid-project | When client requests work outside the original SOW |

### Freelancer / Contractor Contracts

| # | Template | Purpose | When Used |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5 | **Freelancer / Independent Contractor Agreement** | Defines the relationship, payment terms, IP assignment, confidentiality | Signed before any freelancer starts work |
| 6 | **IP Assignment Addendum** | Ensures all work product created by freelancers belongs to Crelligent (and ultimately the client) | Attached to freelancer agreement |

### Platform / SaaS Contracts

| # | Template | Purpose | When Used |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 7 | **SaaS Terms of Service** | Legal terms for VeloDesk, BI Suite, MarketPulse users | Published on website, accepted at sign-up |
| 8 | **Privacy Policy** | How you collect, use, and protect user data (NDPR compliant) | Published on website |
| 9 | **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** | For enterprise clients — how you handle their data | When client requires it (common in Enterprise) |

### Internal / Business Contracts

| # | Template | Purpose | When Used |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 10 | **Partnership / Referral Agreement** | Terms for referral fees (VCs, PE firms, partners) | When formalizing a referral relationship |
| 11 | **Employment Offer Letter** | For Phase 2 when you make your first full-time hires | When hiring full-time |
| 12 | **Employee Handbook / Policies** | Company rules, leave, remote work, code of conduct | Phase 2, before first hire |

### Key Clauses Your Lawyer Must Include

| Clause | Where | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IP Ownership** | MSA, SOW, Freelancer Agreement | Client owns the deliverables; Crelligent owns its methodology and tools |
| **Payment Terms** | MSA, SOW | 50% upfront, net-14 on balance; late payment penalties |
| **Scope Limitations** | SOW | Only what's in the SOW is delivered; everything else is a change order |
| **Termination** | MSA, Freelancer Agreement | Either party can terminate with 14–30 days notice; payment for work done |
| **Limitation of Liability** | MSA, SaaS ToS | Cap liability at the value of the engagement |
| **Confidentiality** | NDA, MSA, Freelancer Agreement | Mutual; survives termination by 2–3 years |
| **Non-Compete / Non-Solicit** | Freelancer Agreement | Freelancers can't poach your clients |
| **NDPR Compliance** | Privacy Policy, DPA | Nigerian Data Protection Regulation compliance |
| **Dispute Resolution** | All contracts | Mediation first, then arbitration (Lagos) |
| **Force Majeure** | MSA | Protection for events outside your control |
