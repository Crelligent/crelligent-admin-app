# Crelligent — Founder's Reading List

> Curated books for building a systems engineering firm. Organized by what they teach you.

---

## 🏗️ Systems Thinking (Your Core Discipline)

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Thinking in Systems** | Donella Meadows | The bible of systems thinking — feedback loops, leverage points, system behavior |
| **The Fifth Discipline** | Peter Senge | Building learning organizations through systems thinking |
| **An Introduction to General Systems Thinking** | Gerald Weinberg | How to think about complex systems at a fundamental level |
| **Systemantics (The Systems Bible)** | John Gall | Why complex systems fail — essential for anyone designing them |

---

## 🚀 Startups & Company Building

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Lean Startup** | Eric Ries | Build-measure-learn — the foundation of Foundry's methodology |
| **Zero to One** | Peter Thiel | Creating something new vs copying — monopoly thinking |
| **The Hard Thing About Hard Things** | Ben Horowitz | The brutal reality of running a company — no sugarcoating |
| **Blitzscaling** | Reid Hoffman | When and how to scale aggressively |
| **The Mom Test** | Rob Fitzpatrick | How to talk to customers without getting lied to — essential for discovery |

---

## 📐 Engineering & Product

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A Philosophy of Software Design** | John Ousterhout | Complexity management in software — directly applies to your architecture work |
| **Designing Data-Intensive Applications** | Martin Kleppmann | The definitive reference for building reliable, scalable systems |
| **The Pragmatic Programmer** | Hunt & Thomas | Core engineering mindset — every engineer you hire should read this |
| **Inspired** | Marty Cagan | How to build products people love — essential for VeloDesk, BI Suite, MarketPulse |
| **Shape Up** | Ryan Singer (Basecamp) | How to scope and ship — alternative to Scrum, better for small teams |

---

## 💼 Sales & Business Development

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Challenger Sale** | Dixon & Adamson | Selling by teaching, not by relationship — perfect for systems engineering sales |
| **SPIN Selling** | Neil Rackham | Asking the right questions to close complex B2B deals |
| **$100M Offers** | Alex Hormozi | Crafting offers so good people feel stupid saying no |
| **Predictable Revenue** | Aaron Ross | Building a repeatable sales machine — outbound playbook |
| **Obviously Awesome** | April Dunford | Positioning — how to make your market understand what you do |

---

## 🧠 Strategy & Leadership

| Book | Author | Why Read It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Good Strategy Bad Strategy** | Richard Rumelt | What strategy actually is (and isn't) — cuts through the noise |
| **The Effective Executive** | Peter Drucker | Managing yourself before managing others — timeless |
| **High Output Management** | Andy Grove | The operating manual for running a company — meetings, decisions, output |
| **Playing to Win** | Lafley & Martin | Strategy as a set of integrated choices — where to play, how to win |
| **The Innovator's Dilemma** | Clayton Christensen | Why great companies fail — understanding disruption |
