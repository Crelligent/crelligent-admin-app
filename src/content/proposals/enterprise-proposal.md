## 1. Executive Summary

Your organization has grown. But your systems — the technology, the operating model, the decision structures — were designed for a different era.

Most transformation programs fail because they treat symptoms (a new CRM, an "Agile" rollout, a cloud migration) without redesigning the **underlying system.**

Crelligent's Enterprise System Redesign Engagement (ESRE)™ is a disciplined, end-to-end partnership that diagnoses the real problem, architects the right solution, builds it alongside your team, and installs the feedback loops that keep it evolving.

We don't just consult. We engineer.

| What You're Experiencing | What's Really Happening |

## 3. Our Approach: The ESRE™ Framework

The ESRE™ is a four-phase engagement that moves from diagnosis through to ongoing evolution:

### Phase 1: The Diagnostic
**Duration:** 4–6 weeks
**Investment:** $50,000–$100,000

We map the **truth** of how your organization behaves — not how it thinks it behaves.

| Activity | Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Executive interviews (C-suite + department heads) | Stakeholder Insights Report |
| End-to-end system mapping | Enterprise System Map |
| Customer & operational signal review | Constraint & Leverage Point Analysis |
| Technology landscape assessment | Risk & Failure Modes Brief |
| Data flow and decision chain analysis | Clear Problem Framing Document |

**Outcome:** You'll know exactly where your system is misaligned — and what to fix first.

> This phase can be engaged standalone. Many clients begin here before committing to the full ESRE™.

---

### Phase 2: The Architecture
**Duration:** 6–10 weeks
**Investment:** $75,000–$150,000

We redesign the core of your business to align completely with your strategic intent.

| Activity | Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business system redesign | Target System Architecture |
| Experience & product alignment | Experience & Product Blueprint |
| Data & decision intelligence design | Decision & Intelligence Model |
| Governance & operating model definition | Operating Model Blueprint |
| Technology & platform roadmap | Engineering & Platform Roadmap |

**Outcome:** A complete target-state architecture — not just a vision, but a buildable plan.

---

### Phase 3: The Build
**Duration:** 12–24 weeks
**Investment:** $100,000–$300,000+

We build the platforms, pipelines, and processes that make the new system your operating reality.

| Activity | Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Platform & product engineering | Working systems & integrations |
| Data pipelines & analytics implementation | Operational dashboards |
| CX implementation | Customer-facing improvements |
| Change enablement & training | Trained internal teams |
| Governance & control installation | Accountability structures |

**Outcome:** Working systems, not PowerPoints. Your team operates within the new architecture.

---

### Phase 4: The Evolution
**Duration:** Ongoing (quarterly retainer)
**Investment:** $10,000–$25,000/month

Static systems die. We install the sensors and feedback loops to ensure your system adapts.

| Activity | Deliverable |
| :--- | :--- |
| Quarterly system health reviews | System Health Reports |
| Feedback loop monitoring | Performance trend analysis |
| Risk & resilience assessments | Early warning triggers |
| Continuous improvement cycles | Improvement Backlog |
| Executive foresight briefings | Strategic adjustment recommendations |

**Outcome:** A living system that improves — not a project that ends and decays.
