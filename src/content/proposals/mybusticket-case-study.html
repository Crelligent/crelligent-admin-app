Mybusticket Nigeria

How Crelligent engineered a complete mobility platform to digitize Nigeria's ₦2.5 trillion interstate bus industry — from system blueprint to market launch.

01 — The Client

Who Is Mybusticket?

Mybusticket Nigeria Ltd is a high-growth mobility-tech startup positioned to become the primary aggregator and booking engine for Nigeria's interstate bus industry.

In a market where 61% of freight and passenger movement occurs by road, the company is transitioning the traditional "park-and-wait" culture into a seamless, mobile-first experience. Through a proprietary mobile application, commuters access real-time seat selection, secure digital payments, and verified travel security — while bus operators gain ERP tools to optimize fleet utilization.

02 — The Challenge

A Broken System, Not a Missing App

Mybusticket didn't need a "booking app." Nigeria's interstate bus market is a complete system failure — fragmented across hundreds of independent operators, thousands of unregulated parks, and millions of cash transactions with zero digital infrastructure.

Information Vacuum

Commuters arrive at parks with zero visibility into schedules, seat availability, or pricing. The average wait is 2–4 hours.

Zero Trust Infrastructure

No verified driver profiles, no vehicle safety records, no digital trail. Passengers board buses with strangers and hope for the best.

Cash Dependence

95%+ of transactions are cash-based. Revenue leakage from agent skimming, ghost passengers, and unrecorded fares is systemic.

Operator Blindness

Bus operators have zero fleet intelligence — no real-time utilization data, no demand forecasting, no route optimization. Paper manifests still dominate.

"The problem wasn't technology. The problem was that the entire interstate bus ecosystem had no system architecture at all. It was entropy in motion."

— Crelligent Engineering Assessment

03 — The Approach

All 9 Capabilities. One Integrated System.

This wasn't an "app build." Crelligent deployed every capability in its arsenal to architect, engineer, and launch a complete operating system for Nigeria's road transport market.

Phase 1 — Define: The System Blueprint Weeks 1–4

Capabilities: Business Design • Product Strategy • Economics & Value Engineering

  • Business Design: Mapped the complete stakeholder ecosystem — commuters, park operators, fleet owners, drivers, agents, regulators. Defined system boundaries and failure modes.
  • Product Strategy: Identified the "Steel Thread" — the minimum viable transaction loop: Search → Select Seat → Pay Digitally → Board with QR Code. Everything else was deferred.
  • Economics: Engineered the revenue model — per-ticket booking fee, operator subscription tiers, and dynamic pricing engine. Modeled the path to break-even within 18 months of launch.
Phase 2 — Build: The Platform Weeks 5–16

Capabilities: CX / Service Design • Operating Model • Technology & Platforms

  • CX / Service Design: Designed the end-to-end commuter journey for first-time smartphone users on low-bandwidth networks. Every interaction was optimized for speed, clarity, and trust.
  • Operating Model: Built the agent onboarding workflow, operator enrollment pipeline, dispute resolution SOP, and customer support playbook. Defined team structure and real-time KPI dashboards.
  • Technology: Built the full stack — React Native mobile app (iOS/Android), Next.js operator dashboard, Supabase backend, Paystack/Flutterwave payment integration, and real-time seat management engine.
Phase 3 — Launch & Sustain: Go Live Weeks 17–24

Capabilities: Data & Intelligence • Governance & Risk • Change & Behavior

  • Data & Intelligence: Instrumented the platform for real-time analytics — route demand heatmaps, peak-hour pricing signals, passenger movement patterns, and fleet utilization scores.
  • Governance & Risk: Implemented verified driver/vehicle profiles, passenger safety protocols (trip-sharing with emergency contacts), FRSC regulatory reporting, and payment reconciliation controls.
  • Change & Behavior: Designed the adoption strategy — agent incentive programs, commuter referral systems, park-by-park onboarding campaigns to migrate from cash to digital.
