## 3. Our Key Hardware Platforms

We are developing 7 core hardware platforms tailored to the operational realities of African industries.

### 1. Fleet Intelligence Modules
* **Hardware:** OBD-II / CAN Bus IoT modules for vehicles.
* **Captures:** Fuel usage, driver behavior, route efficiency, engine diagnostics, idle time, GPS tracking.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Bundle with a Fleet analytics platform, fuel fraud detection, and predictive maintenance.
* **Target Market:** Logistics companies, ride-hailing fleets, government fleets, oil & gas field vehicles.

### 2. Smart Fuel Infrastructure Sensors
* **Hardware:** IoT modules installed in fuel pumps, storage tanks, pipelines, and tanker trucks.
* **Captures:** Fuel levels, pump activity, leaks, delivery verification, dispensing accuracy.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Feed data into the Energy Operations Intelligence System to combat fuel theft and leakage.
* **Target Market:** Fuel marketers, depots, logistics companies, regulators.

### 3. Transport Infrastructure Intelligence Nodes
* **Hardware:** Roadside smart nodes with cameras, traffic sensors, air quality monitors, and edge processors.
* **Captures:** Traffic flow, congestion patterns, accident detection, passenger density.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Power the Urban Mobility Intelligence Platform to support transit operations.
* **Target Market:** City governments, transport operators, bus aggregators.

### 4. Retail Intelligence Sensors
* **Hardware:** Small IoT devices installed in stores.
* **Captures:** Shelf stock levels, customer movement, product pick-ups, temperature for perishables.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Drive Retail Operations Intelligence for inventory automation and demand forecasting.
* **Target Market:** Supermarkets, pharmacies, quick service restaurants.

### 5. Cold Chain Monitoring Devices
* **Hardware:** Temperature + humidity sensors for food logistics, vaccine storage, cold trucks, warehouses.
* **Captures:** Temperature, humidity, location tracking, breach alerts.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Form the Cold Chain Intelligence Network to prevent spoilage.
* **Target Market:** Pharmaceuticals, agriculture exports, food distribution.

### 6. Infrastructure Health Sensors
* **Hardware:** Sensors installed in bridges, buildings, dams, rail lines.
* **Captures:** Vibration, stress, structural shifts, corrosion.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Enable Infrastructure Stability Monitoring for critical assets.
* **Target Market:** Construction companies, governments, mining firms, rail operators.

### 7. Agricultural Field Intelligence Sensors
* **Hardware:** Field devices.
* **Captures:** Soil moisture, rainfall, crop health, sunlight, nutrient levels.
* **Crelligent Strategy:** Build the Precision Agriculture Intelligence platform.
* **Target Market:** Commercial farms, fertilizer companies, insurance firms, governments.

## 4. The Crelligent Node

Like Nvidia created the Jetson platform for embedded AI, Crelligent builds the **Crelligent Intelligence Node (CIN)**: a small industrial device that includes a microcontroller, sensor interface, connectivity (4G/5G/WiFi), and edge AI capability. 

The software layer, our **Enterprise Sensor Intelligence SDK**, allows developers to easily connect fuel sensors, fleet trackers, industrial sensors, and energy meters, and then stream data directly into enterprise systems.
