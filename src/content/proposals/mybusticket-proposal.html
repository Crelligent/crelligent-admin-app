01 — Executive Summary

The Opportunity

Mybusticket Nigeria Ltd is positioned to become the primary aggregator and booking engine for Nigeria's ₦2.5 trillion interstate bus industry — a market where 61% of all freight and passenger movement occurs by road, yet remains largely undigitized.

The company is transitioning the traditional "park-and-wait" culture into a seamless, mobile-first experience through a proprietary application offering real-time seat selection, secure digital payments, and verified travel security — while equipping bus operators with ERP tools to optimize fleet utilization.

Crelligent proposes a full-stack systems engagement — deploying all 9 capabilities to architect, build, and launch a complete mobility-tech platform that is not just a booking app, but a sovereign operating system for Nigeria's road transport ecosystem.

02 — The Problem

Why This Market Is Broken

The Commuter Side

  • No visibility. Commuters arrive at parks with zero information about available buses, departure times, or seat availability.
  • No security. No verified driver or vehicle information. No digital trail of the journey.
  • Cash dependency. 95%+ of transactions are cash-based, creating fraud exposure and zero auditability.
  • Wasted time. The average commuter spends 2–4 hours at a park before departure.

The Operator Side

  • No fleet intelligence. Operators have zero real-time visibility into seat utilization, revenue per route, or demand patterns.
  • Manual operations. Ticketing, manifests, and accounting are entirely paper-based.
  • Revenue leakage. Agents skim fares, buses depart half-empty, and ghost passengers are common.
  • No compliance infrastructure. Regulatory reporting is ad-hoc and unreliable.
