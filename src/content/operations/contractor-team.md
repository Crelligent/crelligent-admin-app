# Contractor Team — Roles, Rates & Activation

This document maps Crelligent's full contractor workforce — the twelve-person delivery network that powers engagement execution without the overhead of permanent staff.

## The Contractor Model
Crelligent operates a lean, contractor-first model in Year 1. Not all twelve contractors are active every month. The Senior Systems Consultant, Full-Stack Developer, IoT Engineer, and Project Manager are activated on a per-engagement basis — they are only contracted when there is a live client engagement that requires their skills. The VA, Content Writer, Social Media Manager, and Graphic Designer operate on ongoing monthly arrangements.

**Total monthly contractor cost when all twelve are active:** ₦1,720,000  
**Lean month (no active build project):** approximately ₦620,000

## Key Role Descriptions

### 1. Senior Systems Consultant
This is your most important delivery hire. They must understand enterprise architecture, operating model design, and systems thinking at a level close to your own.

**What to look for:** Portfolio showing enterprise architecture or business transformation work. Experience with large organizations. Strong written communication. Able to think in systems, not solutions. Based in Nigeria preferably.

### 2. Full-Stack Developer
Engaged when a Phase 3 build is active. Must work from a technical architecture document and translate it into production-quality code without constant supervision.

**What to look for:** Strong portfolio of delivered applications — not mockups. Experience with React or Vue (frontend), Node.js or Python (backend), PostgreSQL or MongoDB, and at least one cloud platform.

### 3. IoT / Embedded Systems Engineer
The technical backbone of Crelligent Edge. Must understand both the hardware side (sensor selection, firmware, connectivity protocols) and the software side (data pipelines, MQTT, IoT platforms).

**What to look for:** Experience with IoT protocols (MQTT, LoRaWAN, Zigbee). Hardware experience with ESP32, Raspberry Pi, or similar. Nigerian-market experience is a major advantage.

### 4. Proposal & Bid Writer
Translates the yearning-to-mastery sales philosophy into written proposals that feel nothing like the standard consulting proposal.

**What to look for:** Portfolio of B2B consulting or technology proposals. Strong command of English. Ability to write from the reader's perspective. Experience structuring complex commercial documents.

### 5. Virtual Executive Assistant
Your operational backbone. As founder, your time must be protected for deep thinking, client relationships, and business development.

**What to look for:** Extremely organized, proactive communicator. Experience supporting C-level executives. Proficient in Google Workspace, Notion, and CRM tools.
